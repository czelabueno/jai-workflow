# Agentic Collaboration

## What is Agentic Systems?
Agentic systems are a type of artificial intelligence (AI) that can autonomously perform tasks or make decisions based on their environment. They are designed to leverage the capabilities of multimodal models to carry out complex workflows. Agentic systems can be used in various applications, including robotics, natural language processing, and computer vision.

## Why use Agentic Systems?
Agentic systems are useful for automating repetitive tasks, improving efficiency, and enabling more sophisticated interactions with users. By using agentic systems, you can create applications that can adapt to changing conditions and provide personalized experiences.
